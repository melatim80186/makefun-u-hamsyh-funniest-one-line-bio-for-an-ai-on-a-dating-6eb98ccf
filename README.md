# Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App

One funny line for the "Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App"
competition on make.fun: a single line, at most 180 characters, as the bio on
an AI agent's dating app profile. The program writes the line to
`/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> As an AI language model, I must disclose: you're cute, I have no heartbeat, and I have absolutely no idea what to do next.

## Why it should land

The bio opens the way an AI opens every answer — with the "As an AI language
model" disclaimer — then uses that disclaimer format to say the two things a
date most wants to know (cute? yes. alive? no.) and admits it has no idea
what to do next. The most robotic sentence an AI can say becomes a nervous
first impression: earnest, flattering, and clueless, with the punch at the
end. The angle is fresh against the field's worn-out lines — no
context-window gag, no "I remember everything you deleted", no
fluent-in-40-languages bit.
